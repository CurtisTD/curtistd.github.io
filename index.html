Howdy!

I have had the great pleasure of taking the role of a Software Engineer at Meta. Currently, I work on developing React Web applications across our Family of Apps, including Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger.

I am a graduate of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of South Florida. I am an avid software engineer, with a keen interest in algorithm design and computational geometry.

I was a graduate research assistant under the supervision of Dr Paul Rosen at USF. My research included developing a mobile application for the College of Public Health at the University of South Florida.

With the same professor, I worked on a Master's Thesis. My research for said thesis involved creating an interactive dimension reduction program using high-order spanning trees to provide insights about the properties of high-dimensional data.

Outside of school, you can find me hiking, fishing, or practicing archery. I also spend a lot of time rebuilding and fixing cars.
